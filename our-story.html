The first little moments

Where It All Began

It started at CC, somewhere between volleyball games and the random moments in between. At the time, neither of these felt like the beginning of a love story. Looking back, they kind of were.

Memory No. 01

The Longboard Lesson

One of the first moments I remember was Tim asking me if I knew how to longboard. I told him I did, but after breaking my wrist I was scared to get back on one. He let me hold onto his arm to balance and patiently retaught me how to longboard. At the time, it was just a little moment. Looking back, it feels like one of the first pieces of our story.

♡
Memory No. 02

Roof Ball

Another day at CC, I somehow convinced Tim to play a completely made-up game I called Roof Ball. It was ridiculous, fun, and very us before there was even an “us.” Somehow, those random volleyball-day moments kept giving us reasons to spend a little more time together.
